# RoyalBlue54L Feather illustrative preview

These Dashboard/Reviews preview images depict a genuine example design, not BoardReadyOps customer data, an analysis finding, or manufacturing approval.

- Original design: RoyalBlue54L Feather by LordsBoards; upstream KiCad demo modified by the KiCad Project in February 2025.
- Hardware source: https://github.com/LordsBoards/RoyalBlue54L-Feather-Hardware
- KiCad demo: https://gitlab.com/kicad/code/kicad/-/tree/master/demos/royalblue54L_feather
- Source license: CERN Open Hardware Licence v2 — Permissive (CERN-OHL-P-2.0); license terms accompany the upstream source.
- Derived preview assets: apps/web/public/demo/royalblue54l-pcb.webp and royalblue54l-schematic.webp.
- Changes: Rendered the actual RoyalBlue54L-Feather.kicad_pcb with KiCad 10.0.7 in a perspective 3D view; exported the actual nRF54L15 schematic sheet to SVG and cropped it to the circuit of interest. Encoded both to WebP with no invented geometry, components, nets, findings or manufacturing conclusions.

The source project is linked on the user-facing preview, and remains available under its original license. These images are for product onboarding only.
